Frequently asked questions

What is Buy Canadian?

Buy Canadian is a discovery site that helps you find products from Canadian Shopify merchants. We search the Shopify Global Catalog live on the first visit to a category or search. We do not maintain our own product database or cache images on our servers. Your browser may keep a short-lived copy of a category you already opened so sort, price filter, and roaming back do not query the catalog again.

Are all products made in Canada?

No. We search catalog text for “made in Canada” and the French equivalent “fabriqué au Canada” (and common spelling variants). We may show a “Mentions made in Canada” badge when returned product copy includes similar English or French wording. That is not verified manufacturing or customs origin. What you see are items for sale from Canadian sellers shipping within Canada.

Who sells these products?

Each listing is sold by an independent merchant whose online store uses the Shopify platform. When you click through, you leave Buy Canadian and complete checkout on that merchant's store. We are not the merchant of record.

Are prices accurate?

Prices shown here come from live Catalog data at the time of your search so are expected to be accurate. The merchant's storefront may show different prices, promotions, or availability.

Why isn't 🇨🇦 product X from 🇨🇦 store Y shown here?

In order to appear in a category listing here, the product must have the "category" taxonomy selected in the product setup in the merchant's Shopify store. If the category is missing in the product details, the item won't appear in any category here. It may still be discoverable using the Search option.

Shipping and returns

Shipping destinations, costs, and return policies are set by each merchant. Buy Canadian does not ship products or process returns.

Are you affiliated with Shopify?

No. Buy Canadian uses Shopify's Global Catalog API but is an independent project and is not affiliated with Shopify Inc.

Shipping outside Canada

The site currently searches a catalog locked to Canadian buyers and shipping within Canada. Support for other destinations is planned for a future update. If you are a Canadian (or someone who wishes to support Canadian business) living outside of Canada, let me know where you are and I will prioritize adding the option to "ship to" your country when this update takes place.

You may find that merchants of items listed on this site ship outside of Canada. I have limited the site to initially show items which ship within Canada, but that doesn't exclude the possibility that they might also ship elsewhere. It's worth taking a look at their shipping policies.
